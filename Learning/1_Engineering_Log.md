## Lessons Learned

### Lesson 001

Do not create folder structures before implementation actually requires them.

Reason:

It resulted in duplicate frontend structures.

New Rule:

Inspect → Decide → Execute.

---

### Lesson 002

A small, fully completed feature creates more confidence than many partially completed features.
