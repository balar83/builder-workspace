# Stage 4 — Learning Objectives: Understanding Quadrilaterals

Derived from Stage 3 concepts. Grouped by section for traceability.

## Section 1 — Polygons: curves, convexity and regularity
1. Distinguish a polygon (simple closed figure of line segments) from a curve that isn't one.
2. Distinguish a convex polygon from a concave polygon.
3. Distinguish a regular polygon from an irregular polygon.

## Section 2 — Angle sum property of a polygon
4. Calculate the sum of the interior angles of a polygon with n sides using (n − 2) × 180°, including back-solving for n given the sum.
5. Apply the interior angle sum property to find an unknown interior angle when the others are given.
6. State and apply the exterior angle sum property (always 360°) to find an unknown exterior angle, number of sides, or related interior angle.

## Section 3 — Trapezium and kite
7. Identify a trapezium by its one pair of parallel sides and name its parts (bases, legs).
8. Identify a kite by its two pairs of adjacent equal sides and state its diagonal properties.

## Section 4 — Parallelograms and their special cases
9. State and apply the properties of a parallelogram (opposite sides, opposite angles, adjacent angles, diagonals).
10. Distinguish a rhombus, rectangle and square by their defining extra properties (sides, angles, diagonals).
11. Reason about the hierarchy among quadrilaterals (e.g. every square is a rectangle and a rhombus, but not vice versa).

**Curriculum-alignment note:** objectives 1–3 map to NCERT §3.2; objectives 4–6 to §3.3; objectives 7–8 to §3.4; objectives 9–11 to §3.5.
