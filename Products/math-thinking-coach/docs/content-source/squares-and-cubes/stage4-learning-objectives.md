# Stage 4 — Learning Objectives: Squares and Cubes

Grouped by Stage 2/3 section, consolidated into `canonical-topic.json` at Stage 10 export.

## Section 1 — Square numbers and their properties
1. Identify a perfect square and use the unit's-digit rule to rule out non-squares.
2. Verify whether a number is a perfect square using the sum-of-consecutive-odd-numbers property.
3. Find the number of non-perfect-square numbers between two consecutive squares.
4. Construct a Pythagorean triplet using the 2m, m² − 1, m² + 1 form.

## Section 2 — Finding square roots
5. Find the square root of a perfect square by prime factorisation.
6. Find the square root of a perfect square by repeated subtraction of consecutive odd numbers.
7. Estimate the square root of a non-perfect-square number by bracketing between consecutive whole-number squares.
8. Find the smallest number by which a given number must be multiplied or divided to make it a perfect square, and apply this to real-world area/length problems.

## Section 3 — Cube numbers and their properties
9. Identify a perfect cube using prime factorisation (grouping factors in triples).
10. Find the smallest number by which a given number must be multiplied or divided to make it a perfect cube.

## Section 4 — Finding cube roots
11. Find the cube root of a perfect cube by prime factorisation, including for real-world volume/edge-length problems.
