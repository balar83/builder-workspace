# Stage 4 — Learning Objectives: Exponents and Powers

Grouped by Stage 2/3 section, consolidated into `canonical-topic.json` at export.

## Section 1 — Powers with negative exponents
1. Evaluate a power with a negative integer exponent by rewriting it as 1/aᵐ.
2. Find the multiplicative inverse of a power with a negative exponent.
3. Identify the exponent of 10 attached to a given digit in the expanded form of a decimal, including negative exponents for digits after the decimal point.

## Section 2 — Laws of exponents
4. Apply the product law aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ for integer exponents, including negative ones.
5. Apply the quotient law aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ for integer exponents.
6. Apply the power-of-a-power law (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ, including re-expressing a power with one base as a power with a related base.
7. Apply aᵐ × bᵐ = (ab)ᵐ to combine two same-exponent powers with different bases.
8. Apply (a/b)ᵐ = aᵐ/bᵐ to evaluate powers of fractions, including negative exponents.
9. Apply a⁰ = 1 for any non-zero base expression.
10. Simplify expressions that combine two or more laws of exponents in sequence.
11. Find an unknown exponent in an equation of two equal-base powers.

## Section 3 — Standard form for very small numbers
12. Convert a very small decimal number to standard form (k × 10⁻ⁿ, 1 ≤ k < 10).
13. Convert a number given in standard form (negative exponent) back to its usual decimal form.

## Section 4 — Comparing and combining very large and very small numbers
14. Compare two numbers given in standard form by computing the ratio of their magnitudes.
15. Add or subtract numbers given in standard form by first rewriting them with the same power of 10.
