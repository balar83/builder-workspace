## Section 1 — What is a linear equation?
- **Expression vs. equation**: an equation contains an equality (=) sign; an expression does not. *(`le-raw-01`)*
- **Linear expression**: an expression in one variable whose highest power is 1 (e.g. `2x + 1` is linear; `x² + 1` is not). *(`le-raw-01`)*
- **Linear equation in one variable**: an equation formed from a linear expression in exactly one variable. *(`le-raw-01`)*
- **LHS / RHS**: the expressions on the left and right of the equality sign, respectively. *(`le-raw-01`)*
- **Solution of an equation**: the value(s) of the variable for which LHS = RHS; not every value satisfies the equation. *(`le-raw-01`)*
- **Balancing principle**: performing the same operation on both sides of an equation preserves its equality — this is *why* the solving methods below are valid, not just a rule to memorize. *(`le-raw-01`)*

## Section 2 — Solving when the variable is on both sides
- **Both-sides-variable equations**: unlike equations where RHS is just a number, both LHS and RHS may contain the variable (e.g. `2x - 3 = x + 2`). *(`le-raw-02`)*
- **Transposition**: moving a term from one side to the other while reversing its sign is equivalent to — and a shorthand for — subtracting/adding that term on both sides. *(`le-raw-02`)*
- **Clearing a fractional coefficient**: multiplying both sides by a common denominator turns a fractional equation into an integer one before transposing. *(`le-raw-02`, Example 2)*

## Section 3 — Reducing equations to simpler form
- **Clearing denominators via LCM**: multiply both sides by the LCM of all denominators present, so every term becomes an integer coefficient. *(`le-raw-04`, Example 16)*
- **Expanding brackets before collecting terms**: distribute multiplication over bracketed sums first, then transpose and combine like terms. *(`le-raw-04`, Example 17)*
- **Verifying a solution**: substitute the found value back into the *original* LHS and RHS independently and confirm they're equal — this is the "Check" step modeled in both worked examples. *(`le-raw-04`)*
